fork stats

Privacy-respecting pageview analytics. No cookies, no personal data — unique visitors are a daily-rotating anonymous hash, location is country-level only.

1. Add this line to the <head> of any site, using your client slug:

<script defer data-website="your-slug" src="https://analytics.fork.studio/beacon.js"></script>

2. View the dashboard at stats.fork.studio/your-slug — supports ?days=7|30|90. Hits appear within about 30 seconds.

Localhost traffic is ignored. Works with client-side routed sites (pushState/replaceState/popstate). Email events (Resend) are collected separately in the same store.
